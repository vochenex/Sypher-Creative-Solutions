Moko

A smiling cat and rounded lettering give Moko a playful, friendly character. The identity pairs a red primary logo with pink variations, a standalone cat brandmark, and a compact m-shaped submark. The same soft shapes carry through the color palette and typography.

Stride

A running figure and italic wordmark give Stride a sense of forward movement. Red, black, ivory, and gray support a bold visual system, with a standalone runner and a circular submark. Eurostile and Montserrat extend the identity into its type system.

Fleura

An expressive serif wordmark and botanical motif give Fleura a soft, organic character. Pink and green connect the primary logo, leaf brandmark, and circular submark. Playfair Display and Montserrat balance decorative letterforms with a simple supporting typeface.
